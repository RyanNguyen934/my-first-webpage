Nominations

Nominations can play an important role in the Navy Academy admissions process and strengthen an applicant’s overall profile.

Eligible nomination categories

  • Members of Congress: Each U.S. Representative and Senator can nominate up to five students. You must apply to your congressional district’s Representative and to both Senators.
  • Vice President: Up to five students at a time can be appointed from this category.
  • Navy & Marine Corps (Active & Reserve): 170 appointments annually for enlisted personnel.
  • ROTC/JROTC Units: Each unit can nominate three candidates.
  • Children of Deceased/Disabled Veterans, POW, MIA: Up to 65 midshipmen.
  • Children of Medal of Honor Recipients: Special nomination.
  • Presidential Nomination: For children/adopted children of retired or active-duty armed forces personnel (including Coast Guard) meeting service requirements.
