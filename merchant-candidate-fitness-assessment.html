Candidate Fitness Assessment (CFA)

The CFA evaluates physical readiness and helps measure whether an applicant meets the required standards for Academy participation.

Requirements

The Merchant Marine Academy CFA is a physical fitness screening used to assess strength, endurance, agility, and overall readiness for academy training. Candidates must complete all required events and meet the minimum standards established by the Academy.

  • Physical preparedness: The assessment measures strength, cardiovascular fitness, flexibility, balance, and stamina.
  • Event-based testing: Candidates complete the required CFA events under official scoring procedures and timing standards.
  • Minimum performance standards: Applicants must meet the Academy’s published minimums for each event; stronger scores improve competitiveness.
  • Official scoring: Results are evaluated using the official USMMA scoring and requirements published by the Academy.
