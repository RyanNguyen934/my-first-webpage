Medical Exams

Medical exams confirm that candidates meet the physical and health standards required for academy training and service.

USAFA applicants complete their medical evaluation through the Department of Defense Medical Examination Review Board (DoDMERB), not through USAFA directly.

How the process works

  1. After you complete enough of your Candidate Kit, USAFA Admissions forwards your information to DoDMERB.
  2. Follow DoDMERB's instructions to create a DMACS account and complete your medical history questionnaire. Bring the completed form to your physical exam.
    DMACS 2.0 Applicant Portal
  3. After receiving an invitation, use DoDMETS to schedule two appointments: a physical exam and a separate optometry (eye) exam.
    DoDMETS

Timeline and results

The process typically takes at least three weeks. Remedial requests, additional tests, or waiver reviews can extend it to four months. DoDMERB may find you qualified, disqualified, or request remedial information.

If you have not received results within 45 days after your exams, contact DoDMERB.

Helpful reminders

  • Complete your Candidate Kit early so the medical process can begin.
  • Respond promptly to remedial requests and keep copies of submitted forms.
  • Check your email regularly for DoDMERB instructions and your DoDMETS exam invitation.
  • Check your DMACS and USAFA portals regularly for updates.
