Candidate Fitness Assessment (CFA)

The CFA is a six-event physical and motor fitness battery that must be completed in under 40 minutes, with each event scored on a 0–100 scale; meeting the minimum standards is required, but higher scores improve your competitiveness.

Test Structure

The CFA measures muscular strength, endurance, cardio-respiratory fitness, power, balance, and agility. The six events are administered consecutively in less than 40 minutes:

  1. Basketball Throw – Kneeling throw from 10 feet away
  2. Pull-Ups / Flexed Arm Hang – Pull-ups (males) or 12-second flexed arm hang (females)
  3. Shuttle Run – 20-meter agility shuttle
  4. Crunches (Modified Sit-Ups) – Timed abdominal exercise
  5. Push-Ups – Timed upper-body exercise
  6. 1-Mile Run – Cardio endurance test

Note: Meeting only the minimums may not result in a passing overall CFA; the Admissions Board uses scale scores for each event, and higher scores are advantageous.

Administration & Scoring

  • Must be administered by a qualified official (PE teacher, coach, JROTC instructor, active duty officer, or Blue & Gold Officer).
  • Raw scores are converted to 0–100 scale scores per event.
  • Results are submitted electronically to USNA.

Tip: Train to exceed minimums — the CFA is part of a competitive admissions process, and strong performance can strengthen your application.
