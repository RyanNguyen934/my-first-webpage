SAT/ACT & GPA Requirements

For the 2024–2025 admission cycle, USMMA’s median SAT score is 1,200 (600/600) and median ACT score is 25, with an average GPA of about 3.6 for admitted students.

SAT & ACT Scores

  • Median SAT: 1,200 (Evidence-Based Reading & Writing 600, Math 600)
  • SAT 25th percentile: 1,110; 75th percentile: 1,300
  • SAT section averages: Math 600 (550–650), Reading/Writing 600 (560–650)
  • Median ACT: 25
  • ACT 25th percentile: 22; 75th percentile: 29
  • ACT section medians: Math 26, English 24

USMMA accepts either SAT or ACT (highest scores from up to three years before the application deadline). There is no minimum SAT requirement, but scores below ~1,150 are considered less competitive.

GPA

  • Average GPA of admitted students: ~3.6 (weighted, out of 4.0)
  • Some sources list a slightly lower average of 3.15 for all applicants, but the 3.6 figure reflects the more selective admitted cohort.
  • A GPA at or below the average may require a higher test score to compensate.

Acceptance Rate & Selectivity

Acceptance rate: ~34.2%
