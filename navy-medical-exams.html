Medical Exams

The U.S. Naval Academy’s medical exam is administered by the Department of Defense Medical Examination Review Board (DoDMERB) and includes a short physical and a separate vision exam, with results reviewed for compliance with strict service standards.

How the Process Works

  1. Application trigger – Once you progress in your USNA application, USNA sends your name to DoDMERB via the DoDMETS portal.
  2. Scheduling – You’ll receive an email from DoDMETS to book two separate appointments:
    • Physical exam (~15 minutes, no blood draw or urine test)
    • Vision exam at an optometry facility
  3. Review – DoDMERB reviews your medical history, exam results, and any additional records. Outcomes are Qualified, Disqualified, or Remedial.
  4. Waivers – If disqualified for a potentially waivable condition, USNA may start a waiver process if your record is competitive.

What the Physical Exam Includes

  • Height, weight, pulse, blood pressure
  • Review of medical history (illnesses, injuries, surgeries, family history)
  • General health screening; no invasive tests at routine exams

Vision Exam Requirements

  • Glasses/contacts must be removed before the exam:
    • Soft lenses: remove 3 days before
    • Hard/gas permeable: remove 21 days before
    • Orthokeratology: remove rigid lenses 90 days before
  • Vision not correctable to 20/20 in both eyes, or refractive error > ±8.00 D or astigmatism > 3.00 D, is disqualifying.

Common Disqualifying Conditions

  • Uncorrectable vision or severe refractive error
  • Certain chronic illnesses, severe allergies, weight outside military standards, some mental health conditions
  • Other conditions that could impair military duty
