Strong SAT or ACT scores can strengthen a Navy Academy application and show academic readiness.

USNA SAT, ACT, and GPA Requirements

For the 2026–2027 admissions cycle, USNA’s middle 50% of admitted students score SAT 1150–1370 (avg. 1260), ACT 26–32 (avg. 29), and have an average GPA around 3.8.

SAT Scores

  • Middle 50% range: 1150 – 1370
  • Average: 1260
  • Section breakdown (25th–75th percentile):
    • Evidence-Based Reading & Writing: 560 – 680
    • Math: 590 – 690
  • Competitive target: 1340+; 1370+ places you in the top 25% of admitted students.

ACT Scores

  • Middle 50% range: 26 – 32
  • Average: 29
  • Section range:
    • English: 25 – 34
    • Math: 26 – 32

GPA

  • Average GPA of admitted students: ~3.8 on a 4.0 scale
  • Some reports list 3.75–3.8 depending on source and weighting method.
  • USNA is highly selective; most admitted students are in the top 10% of their high school class.

Selectivity

  • Acceptance rate: ~9%
  • Only a small fraction of applicants are admitted, and USNA considers the entire application, including nominations, leadership, and other factors.
