Basic Eligibility

Applicants must be a U.S. citizen, meet age and academic standards, and complete the required preparatory steps for the Air Force Academy application process.

  • Age: Be at least 17 years old and not past your 23rd birthday on July 1 of the year you enter the Academy.
  • Citizenship: Be a United States citizen.
  • Status: Be unmarried with no dependents.
  • Character: Be of good moral character.
