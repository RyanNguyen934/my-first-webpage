Nominations

  • Members of Congress: Both U.S. Senators from your state and your local U.S. Representative.
  • Vice President of the United States: Open to applicants nationwide.
  • Service-Connected Sources: Children of career military personnel, active-duty soldiers, ROTC members, and children of deceased/disabled veterans or Medal of Honor recipients.
    • Eligibility: U.S. citizen, ages 17 to 23 by July 1 of the year you enter, unmarried, and without dependents.
  • Application Period: Usually opens in the spring of your junior year (around May 15).
  • Congressional Deadlines: Most congressional nomination deadlines fall between September and October of your senior year.
  • Materials Needed: Transcripts, SAT or ACT scores, a resume, a 500-word essay, letters of recommendation, and an interview.
