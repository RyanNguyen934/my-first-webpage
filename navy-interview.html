Interview

You cannot directly “book” a US Naval Academy interview — it is scheduled automatically by the Academy’s Admissions Office after you complete your application and are selected for the interview stage.

Step-by-step process

  1. Complete the USNA application
    • Submit your preliminary application and full application through the official USNA admissions portal.
    • This includes your personal data, essays, academic info, and required documents.
    • You must also secure a nomination from a U.S. Representative, U.S. Senator, or the Vice President.
  2. Pass required screening steps
    • USNA will review your application, academic records, and other requirements.
    • You must also complete the Candidate Fitness Assessment (CFA) and pass the DoDMERB medical exam.
  3. Receive interview notification
    • If you are selected for an interview, the Admissions Office will email you with the interview details, including date, time, and location (or virtual link).
    • You do not request or choose the interview date — it is assigned by the Academy.
  4. Prepare for the interview
    • The interview is part of the “whole-person” review, focusing on leadership, character, and fit for the Academy.
    • Review USNA’s interview preparation guidance and practice with common questions.

Key points to remember

  • No self-scheduling: You cannot call or email to set an interview date; it is only offered after you are selected.
  • Check your email: The official notification will come from the USNA Admissions Office.
  • Stay in contact: If you have questions about your interview or need accommodations, you can contact the Admissions Office via the contact info on the USNA website.

Official source: The USNA admissions site states that the interview is one of the steps in the admissions process and is arranged by the Academy after you are selected.

For the most accurate and up-to-date instructions, always refer to the USNA Admissions page and the Candidate Instructions.
