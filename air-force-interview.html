Interview

The interview helps assess leadership potential, motivation, communication skills, and readiness for cadet life.

You do not schedule the USAFA interview yourself. USAFA schedules it after your application materials are submitted and reviewed.

USAFA interviews are often conducted by an Academy Liaison Officer (ALO), usually during your senior year. A separate panel may interview you for a congressional nomination.

What to prepare

  • Explain why you want to attend USAFA and serve as an officer.
  • Be ready to discuss your leadership experiences, the Academy's mission and Honor Code, and how you handle challenges.
  • Practice speaking clearly about your experiences and, if asked, current events.

Interview tips

  • Research USAFA and prepare specific, honest answers rather than memorized responses.
  • Dress neatly, sit with good posture, and make natural eye contact.
  • Thank your interviewer afterward and use any feedback to prepare for future interviews.
