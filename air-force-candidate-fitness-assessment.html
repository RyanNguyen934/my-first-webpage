Candidate Fitness Assessment

The CFA evaluates physical fitness readiness. It includes a series of tests designed to assess strength, stamina, and overall athletic readiness.

Exercise Men’s Average Women’s Average
Basketball Throw 69′ 42′
Pull-ups/Flexed Arm Hang 12 2
Shuttle Run 8.1 seconds 9.4 seconds
Modified Sit-ups (crunches) 81 78
Push-ups 62 41
One-mile Run 6:29 7:30

Who can administer the CFA:

  • Any physical education teacher
  • Service Academy Liaison Officer
  • JROTC instructor
  • Any active duty commissioned officer
  • Any active duty non-commissioned officer (NCO)

It is your responsibility to arrange for a qualified person to conduct your examination.
