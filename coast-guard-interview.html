Interview

Interview process

  • By invitation only: Admissions may ask you to complete an interview to learn more about your goals, preparation, and character.
  • Participation is mandatory if selected: If an admissions representative contacts you for an interview, completing it becomes a required step in your process.
  • Flexible formats: Interviews can take place online, over the phone, or face-to-face, depending on mutual agreement and safety considerations.
  • Who conducts it: You will typically be interviewed by an Admissions Officer or an Academy Admissions Partner, such as a graduate or auxiliary member.
