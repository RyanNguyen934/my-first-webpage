Medical Exams

  • Medical exams for the U.S. Military Academy (USMA) at West Point: are mandatory physical evaluations managed by the Department of Defense Medical Examination Review Board (DoDMERB) at no cost to the candidate.
  • Authorization: You must become an official candidate before DoDMERB emails you instructions to schedule your physical and vision exams near your assigned ZIP code. Private physician examinations are not accepted.
  • Appointments: Expect separate appointments for the general physical (vitals, hearing, medical history) and the eye exam (vision, color perception, depth perception).
  • Timeline: The process takes several months, running generally from July 1 of your rising senior year through April 1 (with remedials cleared by April 15).
  • Vision: Must be correctable to 20/20. Uncorrectable vision is a red flag.
  • Medical Records: Bring documentation for past broken bones, allergies, surgeries, chronic conditions, and recovery notes. For broken bones, bring a doctor's letter stating you have returned to full, rigorous activity without limitations.
  • Common Disqualifications: Conditions like asthma, orthopedic repairs, ADD/ADHD, eating disorders, and past substance abuse or severe mental health history may trigger a disqualification, though individual waivers can be reviewed and granted directly by USMA.

DoDMERB portal: This site is used to manage your medical examination process and schedule the required exams after you are invited to complete them. It is the official system used by candidates for the medical review process.
