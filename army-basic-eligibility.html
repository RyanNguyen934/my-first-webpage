Basic Eligibility

Applicants must be a U.S. citizen, meet age and academic standards, and complete the required preparatory steps for the Army Academy application process.

  • Age: Must be 17 to 22 years old by July 1 of the admission year.
  • Citizenship: Must be a U.S. citizen (except for international exchange cadets).
  • Marital Status: Must be unmarried.
  • Dependents: Must have no legal obligation to support children or other dependents, and must not be pregnant.
