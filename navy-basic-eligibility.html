Basic Eligibility

Applicants must meet the required age, citizenship, academic, and personal standards for the Navy Academy application process.

  • Citizenship: Be a United States citizen by July 1 of the year you enter.
  • Age: Be at least 17 years old and not have passed your 23rd birthday before July 1 of your entry year.
  • Marital status: Unmarried.
  • Dependents: No children or other legal dependents.
  • Social Security: Have a valid Social Security Number (or apply for one).
  • Education: Be in high school (or have completed a GED) and meet academic standards.
