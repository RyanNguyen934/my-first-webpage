Medical Exams

Medical exams confirm that applicants meet the physical and health standards needed for Academy training and service.

You must complete a DoDMERB medical exam before you can be appointed to the Academy, and the exam must be finished by the deadline for your admission type.

How the process works

  1. Confirm your admission type and deadline.
    • Early Action: complete the medical exam by Feb 15.
    • Regular Admission: complete the medical exam by Apr 1.
    • The DoDMERB process opens July 13 for the next incoming class.
  2. Create your Applicant Portal account and complete your online application. The portal stores your DoDMERB results and other required documents.
    Coast Guard Academy Admissions
  3. After you submit your application, the Academy will email you instructions for scheduling your DoDMERB exam.
    • Book your exam through the DoDMERB system.
    • Choose a location and date that works for you.
    • The exam is in-person and includes a physical, vision, hearing, and mental health review.
  4. Prepare for the exam.
    • Bring your photo ID.
    • Bring corrective lenses or hearing aids if needed.
    • Bring a medication list and any relevant medical records.
    • Bring your DoDMERB appointment confirmation.
  5. Complete the Physical Fitness Examination (PFE). This is separate from the DoDMERB medical exam and must be arranged with a qualified adult administrator such as a coach, PE teacher, or JROTC instructor.
  6. Submit your results and wait for approval. After your DoDMERB exam, results are uploaded to your Applicant Portal. The Academy reviews them before you can be appointed.

Helpful reminders

  • Do not skip the DoDMERB exam — without it, you cannot be appointed.
  • Meet the deadline for your admission type; late exams can disqualify you.
  • Use the official DoDMERB process rather than a civilian medical exam.
  • Check your email and portal regularly for Academy instructions and updates.
