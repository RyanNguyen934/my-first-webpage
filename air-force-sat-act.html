SAT/ACT/GPA

Strong academic performance on the SAT or ACT can strengthen an Air Force Academy application and improve competitiveness.

Average SAT: 1230–1440 (middle 50% range) / Average composite roughly 1331–1340.

Average ACT: 26–33 (middle 50% range) / Average composite roughly 29–30.4.

Average High School GPA: about 3.81.

Acceptance Rate: about 14.1%.
