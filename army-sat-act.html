SAT/ACT/GPA

Strong academic performance on the SAT or ACT can strengthen an Army Academy application and improve competitiveness.

Average SAT: 1200–1430 (middle 50% range) / Average composite roughly 1320.

Average ACT: 27–33 (middle 50% range) / Average composite roughly 30.

Average High School GPA: about 4.0 (year over year, appointees average around 3.9 unweighted/weighted depending on reporting).

Acceptance Rate: about 12.5%.
