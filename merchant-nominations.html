Nominations

You must be nominated by a U.S. Representative or U.S. Senator from your state of residence to apply to the U.S. Merchant Marine Academy (USMMA), and nominations must be received by January 31 each year.

How to Get a Nomination

  • Who can nominate you: Only a U.S. Representative or U.S. Senator from your state of residence/domicile can nominate you.
  • You do not have to live in the same congressional district as your nominator, but they must be from your state.
  • USMMA does not accept Vice Presidential, military service-connected, or other non-congressional nominations.

Process

  1. Identify your sponsors: Contact your U.S. Representative and both U.S. Senators.
  2. Apply early: Each office has its own deadline and process; some may require interviews or screening.
  3. Submit a nomination packet: Include academic records, leadership experience, physical fitness info, essays, and recommendations.
  4. Meet deadlines: Nominations must reach USMMA by January 31; late nominations close your application.

Key Points

  • Competitive: Nominations are selective; a nomination does not guarantee appointment.
  • Eligibility: Must be a U.S. citizen, at least 17 and under 25 years old on July 1 of the entry year, unmarried, and without dependents.
  • Secretarial appointments: Up to 50 qualified applicants per year can be appointed without a congressional nomination, but you must meet all academic, physical, and character standards.
