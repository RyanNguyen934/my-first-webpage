Pre-Candidate Questionnaire

This questionnaire helps assess candidate background, motivation, and readiness before the full Navy Academy review process.

USNA Pre-Candidate Questionnaire Requirements

The Pre-Candidate Questionnaire (also called the Preliminary Application) is the first step in the USNA admissions process, used to confirm you meet basic eligibility and to collect essential personal, academic, and activity information before you complete the full application.

Purpose

This questionnaire is not the full application, but it is mandatory to become an official candidate. It screens for:

  • Basic eligibility (citizenship, age, residency)
  • Academic standing and test scores
  • Extracurricular and athletic involvement
  • Physical fitness and medical history
  • Other personal details needed for USNA’s records

If you pass this stage, you’ll receive a candidate number and login for the official application.

Eligibility to Complete

You may submit the Pre-Candidate Questionnaire if you:

  • Will be a senior in high school in October of the entry year, or are already a senior
  • Are a current college student seeking transfer admission
  • Meet USNA’s basic eligibility:
    • U.S. citizen by July 1 of the entry year
    • At least 17 years old and under 23 on that date
    • Unmarried, no dependents
    • Valid Social Security Number

If you already applied to the Naval Academy Summer Seminar (NASS), you do not need to submit the Preliminary Application again.

Information Requested

The questionnaire typically asks for:

  • Personal details: legal name, DOB, sex, height, weight, citizenship type, ethnicity/race, cultural heritage
  • Contact info: email (must be accessible), phone
  • Education: current grade level, high school ETS code, class rank, GPA, SAT/ACT/PSAT scores
  • Activities: extracurriculars, leadership roles, sports/physical activities
  • Other: medical history, criminal record (if any)

How to Complete

  • Available online via the USNA admissions site; takes ~15–30 minutes
  • Must be completed in one session (not saveable)
  • Requires an email address; create one if needed via your school/library
  • Submit as early as possible — earlier applications are reviewed sooner

Tips for Success

  • Start early so you can gather grades, test scores, and activity details before you begin.
  • Double-check your personal and contact information to avoid delays.
  • Be honest and complete in your academic, medical, and extracurricular sections.
  • Submit as soon as you are ready because earlier review can help keep your process moving.
