Physical Fitness Exam (PFE)

The PFE measures physical readiness through three events. Each event is worth up to 100 points, for a maximum score of 300.

Test events

  1. Cadence push-ups: Complete one push-up every two seconds for up to two minutes, to a maximum of 60. Resting is not allowed.
  2. Two-minute sit-ups: Complete as many properly performed sit-ups as possible in two minutes.
  3. 1.5-mile run: Complete a measured 1.5-mile course as quickly as possible. Walking is allowed if needed; treadmills are not allowed.

Events must be completed in order, with no more than five minutes of rest between events.

Applicant benchmark: The manual states applicants who score below 150 points will not receive a full appointment. USCGA may raise this benchmark depending on the applicant pool.

Test administration

  • Ask a coach, physical education instructor, personal trainer, or active-duty military member to administer the exam.
  • Practice each event and warm up before the test.
  • All scores must come from one test session; results from separate sessions cannot be combined.
  • The examiner records your scores and submits the official results to USCGA using the instructions provided.
