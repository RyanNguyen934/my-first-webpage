Candidate Fitness Assessment (CFA)

  • Purpose: Measures strength, agility, power, balance, speed, and endurance to predict physical success at the academy.
  • Weight: 15% of your whole candidate score (based on West Point's 55/30/15 formula: 55% academics, 30% leadership/character, 15% physical fitness).
  • Format: Six consecutive events completed in a strict time schedule, usually under 40 minutes, with designated rest periods between events.
    • Basketball Throw: From a kneeling position (3 trials max; maximum score is 102 ft for men, 68 ft for women).
    • Cadence Pull-Ups or Flexed-Arm Hang: Pull-ups for men (18 reps max); flexed-arm hang alternative for women (7 reps / max score timing).
    • Shuttle Run: 40-yard total via four 10-yard segments (2 trials; max score is 7.8s for men, 8.6s for women).
    • Modified Sit-Ups / Crunches: 2 minutes duration (max score 95 reps).
    • Push-Ups: 2 minutes duration (max score is 75 for men, 50 for women).
    • 1-Mile Run: Cardiovascular endurance test (max score is 5:20 for men, 6:00 for women).
  • Approved Proctors: Must be administered by a physical education teacher, Military Academy Liaison Officer (MALO), Field Force representative, U.S. military officer/NCO, or JROTC/ROTC instructor.
  • Prohibitions: Your test administrator cannot be a relative or a coach.
