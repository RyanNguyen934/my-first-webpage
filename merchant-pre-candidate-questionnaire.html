Pre-Candidate Questionnaire

The Pre-Candidate Questionnaire is the first official step in the USMMA admissions process, used to collect your basic academic, physical, leadership, and maritime-interest information so the Academy can determine if you are a competitive candidate for further review.

What it is

The Pre-Candidate Questionnaire is an online form available on the USMMA website. It is not the full application — it is an initial screening tool. The Academy uses it to:

  • Record your academic background (courses, GPA, test scores if available)
  • Document your physical fitness status and any relevant activities
  • Note your leadership experience and extracurricular involvement
  • Capture your interest in maritime careers and career goals

Once completed, the Academy’s admissions office reviews your responses. If you meet the initial criteria, you will be considered a “competitive candidate” and receive additional forms, instructions, and guidance.
