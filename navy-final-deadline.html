Final Deadline

The final deadline for the United States Naval Academy to receive all application materials for the next class is January 31 of the year before the intended enrollment.

How the timeline works

  • Jan 31 – Recommended to complete your preliminary application during junior year.
  • Mar 1 – Official application opens (junior year).
  • May – Applications ramp up; you may be contacted by a Blue and Gold Officer.
  • Jul 1 – Target date to have your application fully submitted (including nomination, transcripts, test scores, etc.).
  • Oct 15 – Many congressional members/senators have their own nomination application deadlines (varies by nominator).
  • Jan 31 – Final deadline for the Academy to receive all application materials, including nominations, for the next class.

What “final deadline” means

By January 31 (Eastern Time), the Academy must have:

  • Completed application form and candidate portal submission
  • Nomination from an official source (Congressional, Vice President, etc.)
  • Required test scores (SAT, ACT, or CLT starting 2027)
  • Transcript, medical exam, fitness assessment, and interview results

If you miss this date, your application will not be considered for that class.

Tips

  • Always confirm the exact year’s dates on the USNA admissions site before applying, as dates can shift slightly year to year.
  • Submit well before January 31 to allow time for corrections or missing items.
  • All deadlines are in Eastern Time because the Academy is in Annapolis, MD.
