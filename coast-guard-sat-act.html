SAT/ACT

Strong academic performance on the SAT or ACT can strengthen a Coast Guard Academy application and improve admissions competitiveness.

Reported average SAT composite: about 1,312-1,339, depending on the source. Aim for 1,360 or higher to be competitive.

Typical SAT score range:

  • Composite: about 1,240-1,390 for the middle 50% of admitted students.
  • Math: average around 656; middle 50% about 620-692.
  • Reading and Writing: average around 656; middle 50% about 620-700.

GPA context: Reported average weighted GPA is about 3.82. USCGA considers your academic record and the rest of your application, not test scores alone.

Submitting scores: Self-report your scores through the online application. You may also send them through your high school or College Board. The USCGA SAT code is 5807.
