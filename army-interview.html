Interview

  • Requirement: The United States Military Academy (USMA) at West Point requires applicants to complete an admissions interview, typically conducted by a local Field Force Representative (FFR), generally occurring from July through December of your senior year.
  • Field Force Representatives (FFRs): These are local, well-informed graduates or admissions volunteers appointed to advocate for and evaluate candidates.
  • Summer Leaders Experience (SLE) alternative: If you attend the Summer Leaders Experience at West Point between May 29 and June 14 during the summer before your senior year, an official interview conducted during that program may count in place of the standard FFR interview, provided it shows a completed green checkmark in your candidate portal.
  • Timeline: Most interviews take place during the fall of your senior year (July through December), often before your application file is fully completed.
  • Scheduling: You can locate your assigned local Field Force Representative's contact information after you start your online application on the West Point Admissions Site, and it is recommended to proactively reach out if they do not contact you first during the fall semester.
  • Format: The interview typically lasts 30 to 45 minutes and may take place in person (at a neutral location, the representative's office, or your home) or virtually via phone or video platform.
