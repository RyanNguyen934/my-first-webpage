Basic Eligibility

Basic eligibility for the U.S. Merchant Marine Academy requires U.S. citizenship, being 17–24 years old on July 1 of entry year, good moral character, meeting medical and physical standards, and obtaining a Congressional nomination.

Core requirements

  • U.S. citizenship
  • Age: 17–24 years old on July 1 of the entry year
  • Good moral character
  • Medical and physical standards
  • Congressional nomination
